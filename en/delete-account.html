Delete your account

Last updated: 8 November 2026

In the app

  1. Open Psst and your profile/settings.
  2. Open Account → Delete my account.
  3. Read the effects, choose Permanently delete my account, and confirm.
Deletion requires a connection. The app does not require the partner’s approval. The option is also available in settings before pairing.

What is removed

Your account/profile, statuses, notes, nudges/replies, private reminders and account-linked device records are removed from the active service. The relationship, shared list/history and mutual location permissions/points are removed. Your partner’s account and their private reminders remain. Deletion is permanent; provider backups/technical logs follow the retention described in the privacy policy.

Apple sign-in connection

After deletion, you may also remove Psst under Apple Account → Sign-In and Security → Sign in with Apple. Psst provides a link to Apple Account after the account is deleted. Removing that connection is separate from deleting the Psst account.

Cannot access the app?

Email eneskucukpehlivan1@gmail.com with a deletion request. We may need to verify ownership before acting; do not send passwords or someone else’s account details.
