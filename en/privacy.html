Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Who operates Psst?

Psst is operated by Enes Küçükpehlivan. Contact: eneskucukpehlivan1@gmail.com. This policy covers the iOS/iPadOS app, its widgets and Live Activities, and this support website.

What we process

  • Account: your internal user ID, Apple sign-in identity, the email address Apple provides (which may be a private relay address), and the name or nickname you choose.
  • Your shared content: mood/status, optional needs and notes, nudges and replies, shared reminders and completion status, and the pairing invitation/relationship needed to connect you.
  • Optional wellbeing information: a custom status or note can include information about your wellbeing or health. Psst does not access HealthKit or clinical records.
  • Private reminders: reminders marked for yourself are associated with your account and are not made available to your partner.
  • Location, only when enabled: latitude/longitude, reported accuracy and update times. iOS may provide precise or approximate location. Our active database keeps the latest point per person; we do not provide a route-history feature.
  • Device and service operation: APNs push tokens, push environment, selected notification language, delivery/error state, Live Activity tokens and reminder state. Our hosting providers may process IP addresses, request metadata and operational/security logs.

Why we use it

We use this data to authenticate your account, maintain your private pairing, deliver your chosen content, synchronize reminders, provide optional location sharing and notifications, and operate and protect the service. There are no ads, advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking SDKs. We do not sell your personal data.

Who can see shared information?

Only the participants in your confirmed pairing can access relationship content through the app. Location additionally requires both participants to enable sharing and hold valid permissions. Shared content is stored by our backend provider; it is protected in transit by HTTPS, but Psst does not claim end-to-end encryption or that the developer/hosting provider is technically unable to access stored data. Your partner can read, copy or screenshot information you intentionally share.

Location choices

Location sharing starts off. Each person can choose 1 hour, 8 hours, or sharing until stopped. Either person can stop sharing without the other person’s approval; server access then stops for both. A valid connection is needed to notify the server. If you stop while offline, this device stops sending and retries revocation after reconnecting; the other device may temporarily retain the last known point. Background updates depend on iOS permission, battery management, connectivity and device operation. Psst is not an emergency, safety, or guaranteed real-time tracking service.

Apple Intelligence

On compatible devices with iOS 26 or later, enabled Apple Intelligence and a ready model, you can request on-device suggestions. Only the partner-shared mood, need and note are supplied to the local Apple model. Names, account IDs, message history and location are not part of that prompt. Generated suggestions are not saved to our server or sent automatically. Prepared suggestions are used when the model is unavailable. We do not send AI prompts to a third-party cloud AI provider.

Service providers and international processing

We use Supabase for authentication, database and synchronization. Core application data is currently hosted in the Tokyo, Japan region. Apple provides Sign in with Apple, APNs, Maps, widgets and on-device AI under Apple’s applicable terms and policies. This website is served by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process web request information. This website has no analytics scripts, contact forms or advertising cookies.

Retention, unpairing and deletion

Active records remain while needed for your account and features, until replaced or deleted. A shared status expires from the current-status view after 24 hours; its latest stored record may remain until replaced, unpaired or deleted. Unpairing removes the relationship, old invitations, shared events/list data, shared status/notes and location permissions/points, while preserving both accounts and private reminders. Account deletion also removes your profile, private reminders and account-linked device records from the active service. Limited provider backups and operational/security records may remain under the providers’ retention schedules; they are not made available to your former partner through the app. Offline devices and already delivered notifications/screenshots cannot be remotely recalled; local cleanup takes effect when those devices refresh.

Your controls and requests

Use Settings → Partner connection to unpair, Settings → Account → Delete my account to delete, the location page to stop sharing, and Settings to mute partner notifications. You can revoke iOS permissions independently. For access, correction, deletion or other privacy requests, contact eneskucukpehlivan1@gmail.com. Please do not email passwords, precise location or identity documents unless a secure verification process is specifically agreed.

Age and changes

Psst is intended for people aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly invite children under 16 to create accounts. If you believe a person under 16 has supplied personal information, contact us so we can investigate and remove it where appropriate. Where local law requires parent/guardian authorization, that authorization is required. We will update this policy when practices change and identify the latest date above.
