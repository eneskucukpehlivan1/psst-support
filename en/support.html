Support

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Contact

For support, privacy requests or reports of unwanted behavior, email eneskucukpehlivan1@gmail.com. Include the app version, iOS version, device model and a short description. Do not include passwords or your partner’s private messages/location unless you intentionally choose to share them.

Pair with your partner

Sign in with Apple, create your space and share the invitation code. Your partner enters the code, and the inviting person confirms the request. Neither person can see relationship data before confirmation. Both partners need Psst on a compatible iPhone or iPad; there is no Android app in this release.

Notifications and language

Allow notifications in iOS Settings and connect notifications in Psst settings. Choose System, Türkçe or English in Settings → Language. On current versions, preset nudges and preset replies use the recipient’s saved language; custom text is delivered as written. Both senders should update to the latest version. If you change language offline, notification language updates when the device reconnects.

Location and background permission

Both partners must enable sharing. Choose 1 hour, 8 hours or until stopped. “Allow Once” is not ongoing background permission. For background updates, allow location while using the app, then use Psst’s background-permission option to request Always, if iOS offers it. Each person can stop sharing at any time. The map shows last-update time and accuracy because iOS does not guarantee continuous updates.

Apple AI is not ready

Apple Intelligence requires supported hardware and iOS 26 or later; not every iPad/iPhone supports it. Enable Apple Intelligence in iOS Settings, ensure device and Siri languages match, and connect to Wi-Fi and power while the model downloads. Psst cannot force that download. Prepared suggestions remain available.

Unpair or mute

Open the profile/settings button → Partner connection → Unpair. The other person’s approval is not required. Shared relationship data and location access end; personal accounts/reminders remain. Mute partner notifications in Settings if you only want a pause. For unwanted or abusive contact, unpair and email support; an old invite cannot reconnect you after removal.

Delete your account

See the account deletion instructions. Signing out or uninstalling the app alone does not delete the server account.

Availability

Psst requires iOS/iPadOS 17 or later and an internet connection for account and shared-data operations. Widgets and Live Activities are scheduled by iOS. No response-time guarantee is offered for this personal support mailbox.
